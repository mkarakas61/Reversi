Account and data deletion

Reversi (Android) · Last updated: 20 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your Reversi account and the data attached to it.

How to request deletion

In the app

Open Settings → App & Account → Delete my account. Once you confirm, your account and the data listed below are deleted; no e-mail is needed.

By e-mail

If you cannot reach the app, write to reversi.destek@gmail.com from the Google account address you play with — we use that to verify the request. Requests are completed within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

  • Your account (Firebase identifier, name, profile photo URL, e-mail address)
  • Your player profile: trophies, rank, coin balance and all online statistics
  • Your match history (each game's result, score, trophies and coins)
  • Your entries in the weekly and all-time leaderboards
  • Any pending matchmaking record

What is kept

  • Completed match records: a match belongs to two players. Your opponent's record of it is kept so the result stays verifiable. Your name and photo are removed from these records, leaving only an anonymous identifier and the board data. They are kept for at most 12 months.
  • Analytics data: measurement data is held in aggregate, not linked to your account, and expires under Google's retention settings (at most 14 months).
  • Data on your device: your settings, saved offline game and offline statistics live on your phone, not on our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.

Deletion is permanent

Once deleted, trophies, rank, coin balance and match history cannot be restored. Signing in again with the same Google account creates a new, empty profile.
