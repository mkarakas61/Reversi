Privacy Policy

Reversi (Android) · Last updated: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what data the Reversi mobile game collects, why, and what happens to it. It describes the app's actual behaviour as implemented in its source code.

1. Who is responsible

Mustafa Karakaş (individual developer)
Contact: reversi.destek@gmail.com

2. Short version

  • You can play without signing in. Single-player and two-player (same device) games run entirely on your phone; no game data leaves it.
  • Online play requires either a Google sign-in or a guest (anonymous) session.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with ad networks. The app shows no ads.
  • You can have your account deleted: see Account deletion.

3. Data we collect

3.1 Account data (only if you sign in)

DataSourceStored in
User ID (uid)Generated by FirebaseFirebase Auth + game database
Display nameYour Google accountFirebase Auth + game database
Profile photo URLYour Google accountFirebase Auth + game database
E-mail addressYour Google accountFirebase Auth only (never written to the game database)

A guest session collects no name, e-mail or photo: it uses an anonymous identifier and a nickname generated on your device. No permanent profile record is created for guests.

3.2 Game data (only if you play online)

  • Your profile: wins, losses, draws, win streaks, total discs flipped, best score margin, trophy count, rank and coin balance.
  • Your match history: for each online game, the result, score margin, discs flipped, trophies gained or lost, coins earned and the date.
  • Match records: the board state and moves of a game, both players' identifiers and the name/photo shown at the time.
  • Matchmaking record: while looking for an opponent, your identifier, name, photo and a summary of your stats are held in a temporary record that closes once you are matched.
  • Leaderboard: the weekly and all-time tables list your name, photo, wins and trophies.

Scores, trophies and coins are calculated only on the server — the app has no permission to write those values itself, which is what keeps results fair.

3.3 What other players can see

While you play online, your name, profile photo, rank, trophies and basic statistics are shown to your opponent, and anyone viewing the leaderboard can see them once you appear on it. If you would rather not be listed, play as a guest — guests are never written to the leaderboard.

3.4 Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played and to find bugs. The events it sends are: game started (language, mode, difficulty), move played (board position and number of discs flipped), forced pass, game ended (scores and winner), and language changed.

Google Analytics additionally collects information such as device model, operating system version, approximate location (country/region level), app version, an app instance identifier and the Android advertising ID (AAID). The app shows no advertising; the advertising ID is present only as a standard part of the measurement library.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android under Settings → Google → Ads.

3.5 Data that stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone: language choice, theme, board and disc selections, sound and music settings, game speed, your saved offline game, your offline statistics, your guest nickname and whether you have seen the intro tour. Uninstalling the app removes them.

4. Why we process this data

  • To run the game and match you with opponents.
  • To keep results, trophies, ranks and the leaderboard accurate and fair, and to prevent cheating.
  • To find bugs and improve the game.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and do not share it for marketing. The game relies on these Google services, which process the data on our behalf:

ServicePurpose
Firebase AuthenticationGoogle and guest sign-in
Cloud FirestoreStoring profile, match and leaderboard data
Cloud FunctionsServer-side score, trophy and coin calculations
Google Analytics for FirebaseUsage measurement and diagnostics
Google Sign-InSigning in with a Google account

The game database and server functions are hosted in the European Union (Belgium, europe-west1). Because of how Google's infrastructure works, some data — analytics in particular — may also be processed on servers in other countries. We may have to disclose data to authorities where the law requires it.

6. Retention

Account and game data are kept until you ask us to delete them. Deleting your account removes your profile, match history and leaderboard entries; see Account deletion for details. Abandoned match records are cancelled automatically. Analytics data follows Google's retention settings (at most 14 months).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (KVKK in Türkiye, GDPR in Europe) you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, object to its processing, and receive a copy of it. To exercise any of these, write to reversi.destek@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days at the latest.

8. Children

Reversi is not designed for children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it — please contact us if you are aware of such a case.

9. Security

Data is processed on Google's infrastructure and encrypted in transit. Database security rules let a player write only their own identity fields; score, trophy and coin fields can be changed only by the server.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Significant changes are also announced inside the app.
